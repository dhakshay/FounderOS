# Pulp-based ice cream

Started 2026-11-24. Ice cream with real fruit pulp as the main flavour base.

## Decisions (operator, 2026-11-24)
- Location: Vizianagaram, Andhra Pradesh, India
- Scale: small manufacturing plant; operator works alone at first
- Channels: packed tubs; quick commerce (Instamart, Blinkit, others)
- Type: dairy
- Budget: Rs 4-15 lakh
- Supply: mango and other pulps, about Rs 200/kg on average
- Premises: available. No 3-phase power yet: use single-phase equipment first, apply to APEPDCL for 3-phase if needed
- Milk, cream, powder: buy wherever cheapest

## Starting recipe (per 10 kg mix, estimates, verify with trials and quotes)
| Ingredient | kg | Rs/kg | Rs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fruit pulp (20%) | 2.00 | 200 | 400 |
| Unsalted butter (10% fat) | 1.25 | 550 | 688 |
| Skim milk powder (10% MSNF) | 1.00 | 300 | 300 |
| Sugar | 1.20 | 45 | 54 |
| Stabiliser/emulsifier (0.4%) | 0.04 | 600 | 24 |
| Water | 4.51 | 0 | 0 |
| **Mix cost** | 10.00 | | **about 1,466 (Rs 147/kg)** |

- 500 ml tub at about 100% overrun = about 270 g of mix = about Rs 40 ingredients (pulp about Rs 11)
- Add packaging about Rs 15 and power/labour/overhead about Rs 8-10: about Rs 65 per tub
- Quick commerce: MRP Rs 199 incl. GST, platform margin about 25-30%, cold-chain to dark store about Rs 8-15: about Rs 45 contribution per tub
- Fixed monthly cost estimate about Rs 65,000 (power, 2 helpers, compliance, misc): break-even about 1,450 tubs a month (about 50 a day)

## Timing
Ice cream demand peaks March-June. Plan: licences and equipment Dec-Jan, pilot batches Feb, launch early March. Mango pulp is available year-round if canned/frozen; fresh seasonal fruits (custard apple Sept-Dec, jackfruit Apr-Jul) suit limited editions.
