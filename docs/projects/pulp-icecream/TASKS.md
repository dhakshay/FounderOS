# Pulp-based ice cream

Started 2026-11-24. Ice cream with real fruit pulp as the main flavour base.

## Decisions (operator, 2026-11-24)
- Location: Vizianagaram, Andhra Pradesh, India
- Scale: small manufacturing plant
- Channels: packed tubs; online
- Type: dairy
- Budget: Rs 4-15 lakh
- Supply: has pulp access at Rs 200/kg

## Open questions
1. Which fruit is the Rs 200/kg pulp (mango, custard apple, jackfruit, other)? Is it seasonal or year-round?
2. Premises: own or rented space? Power load available (3-phase)?
3. Where do you buy milk, cream, SMP (local dairy, Vijaya/Heritage, wholesale)?
4. "Online" = own website/WhatsApp orders, or Swiggy/Zomato/Instamart listings?
5. Team: you alone, or partners/staff with dairy or food experience?

## Rough numbers (estimates, verify with quotes)
- Pulp at Rs 200/kg, 20% of mix: about Rs 11 per 500 ml tub
- Dairy + sugar + stabiliser per 500 ml tub: about Rs 12-16
- Tub, lid, label, carton share: about Rs 12-18
- Total variable cost: about Rs 40-50 per 500 ml tub; premium pulp ice cream sells Rs 150-250
- Cold-chain and distribution will take the largest share of margin
