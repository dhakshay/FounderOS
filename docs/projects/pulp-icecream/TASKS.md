# Pulp-based ice cream

Started 2026-11-24. Idea: ice cream made with real fruit pulp (mango, guava, custard apple, jackfruit, etc.) as the main flavour base, rather than essences or syrups.

## Open questions (operator)
1. Country / city, and the licence regime that applies.
2. Scale: home/cloud kitchen, small shop, or a manufacturing plant?
3. Channel: retail scoops, packed tubs, B2B (restaurants, hotels), or online?
4. Dairy-based, vegan, or both? Sugar-free option?
5. Budget and who is on the team.
6. Do you already have a pulp supplier or fruit access?

## Steps
- [ ] Scope and market: target customer, 5 local competitors, price band
- [ ] Flavours: pick 2-3 seasonal pulps, sourcing and cost per kg
- [ ] Recipe: pulp %, sugar, fat, stabiliser, overrun; cost per litre
- [ ] Compliance: food licence, FSSAI-type registration, cold-chain rules
- [ ] Equipment: batch freezer, hardening freezer, display/transport freezer
- [ ] Pilot batch and 20-person taste test
- [ ] Pricing, packaging, brand, first channel launch
